# Python_.Analysing-and-Tracking-the-Sentiment-and-Topics-on-Social-Media

## Background
Social media is the giant treasure trove containing massive information which is collectively
created by millions of individuals. With the development of text analysis and Natural Language
Processing techniques, social media analysis could go further to discover the sentiment and
topics in text. In this report, we will analyse the submissions about digital nomad in Reddit.
Digital nomad is a new lifestyle that has become more popular since Covid-19 pandemic. This
report provided the insights of the trending concepts and topics, and also the perceptions and
feelings associated with digital nomad. We could discover new business opportunities from this
market research in this booming lifestyle across the globe.

## Data Collection
Firstly, the Reddit API PRAW is employed to gather latest submissions and comments from the
digital nomad subreddit. The total number of submissions, which consists of 986 posts and
22,372 comments, was collected between 23rd February 2025 and 11th April 2025. All analyses
in this report are based on this dataset. The whole text corpus from this dataset is mainly
constructed by three components, including title and content of submission by the author, and
also comments from other users.
In addition, the created timestamp of each submission and comment is recorded. In Reddit
submission, the score records the number of upvotes for the submission that represents the
submission is recognised by other users.
